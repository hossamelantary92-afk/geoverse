## Active Oceania card and header photo (v378)

The active Oceania photograph is a **real photograph of the turquoise Bora Bora lagoon and volcanic mountains** from the Bora Bora airport area in French Polynesia. Photographer: **Elizabeth.guterson**, 4 June 2017. Wikimedia Commons file [Bora Bora Airport.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bora_Bora_Airport.jpg), original resolution **5472 × 3648**, licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Direct original photo URL: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/13/Bora_Bora_Airport.jpg . The image is loaded directly from Wikimedia in the Oceania card and the corresponding header, because the archival workflow has previously failed. The local archive `oceania.webp` may still contain an older image until archive generation succeeds. Do not portray this shot as an aerial photograph; it is a real landscape photo captured at Bora Bora airport.
